# Review Response Template

A simple, behaviorally sound response template for customer reviews.

## The 4-step structure

1. **Acknowledge & validate** — name the feeling, confirm you heard them.
2. **Fix the specific** — what you'll do about that one thing.
3. **Make it easy** — give a contact / next step.
4. **Thank them** — for the feedback (it's free market research).

---

### 5-star review

> "Great food, friendly staff, fast service!"

> "Thanks for the 5 stars — we're glad the roast beef and chips hit the spot today. If you ever want to skip the line, order online here: [link]. Your table's waiting."

---

### 3-star review (constructive)

> "Good food, but the fries were a bit soggy."

> "Thanks for the honest note. We've tightened our fryer timing and are rechecking fry time with the team. Next visit, we'll have you a crisp one. Hope to see you soon!"

---

### 1-star review (complaint)

> "Food was cold and service was slow."

> "We're sorry to hear it. Cold food isn't our standard — please DM us or call [phone] with your name so we can make it right. We'd appreciate the chance to restore your confidence."

---

## Rules of thumb

- **Match the tone.** Calm, not defensive. Even if you disagree.
- **Never argue.** You can acknowledge + fix without admitting fault you don't accept (just don't over-agree on facts).
- **Show action.** "We'll do X" beats "We understand you."
- **Keep it public** until the customer goes private — then continue privately.
